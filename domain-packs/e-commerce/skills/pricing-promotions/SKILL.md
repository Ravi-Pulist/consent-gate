# Pricing & Promotions

## When to Activate
- When working on e-commerce projects that involve pricing rule engines, discount/coupon systems, or promotional campaign management
- When implementing dynamic pricing, bundle pricing, or tiered discount structures

## Core Principles
### 1. Deterministic Promotion Stacking
Define clear, deterministic rules for how multiple promotions interact (stackable vs. exclusive, application order, maximum discount caps). Ambiguous stacking rules lead to unintended deep discounts, margin erosion, and customer confusion when promotions behave inconsistently.
