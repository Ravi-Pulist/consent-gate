# E-Commerce Analytics

## When to Activate
- When working on e-commerce projects that involve conversion funnel analysis, customer lifetime value calculation, or cart abandonment tracking
- When building analytics dashboards, cohort analysis, or A/B testing frameworks for retail metrics

## Core Principles
### 1. Track the Full Funnel
Instrument every step of the customer journey from landing to post-purchase. Gaps in funnel tracking make it impossible to identify where customers drop off. Use consistent event schemas across all touchpoints so funnel analysis is reliable and comparable over time.
