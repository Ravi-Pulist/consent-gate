# SaaS Analytics & Metrics

## When to Activate
- When working on SaaS projects that involve revenue metrics (MRR/ARR), churn analysis, or product usage analytics
- When building dashboards for cohort analysis, NPS tracking, or customer health scoring

## Core Principles
### 1. Standardize Metric Definitions
MRR, churn, and retention must have precise, documented definitions that the entire organization agrees on. Different teams calculating churn differently (logo vs. revenue, gross vs. net) leads to conflicting dashboards and misaligned decisions.
