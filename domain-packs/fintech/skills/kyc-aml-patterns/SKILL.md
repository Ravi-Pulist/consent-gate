# KYC/AML Patterns

## When to Activate
- When working on fintech projects that involve customer identity verification, sanctions screening, or suspicious activity monitoring
- When implementing onboarding flows that require KYC checks or ongoing AML compliance

## Core Principles
### 1. Risk-Based Approach
KYC/AML controls should be proportional to risk. Apply enhanced due diligence for high-risk customers, geographies, and transaction patterns while keeping low-risk onboarding frictionless. One-size-fits-all verification creates unnecessary friction without improving compliance.
