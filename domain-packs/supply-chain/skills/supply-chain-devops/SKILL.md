# Supply Chain DevOps

## When to Activate
- When working on supply chain projects that involve high-availability order processing infrastructure, EDI monitoring, or SLA tracking
- When designing deployment and monitoring strategies for logistics-critical systems

## Core Principles
### 1. Zero-Downtime for Order Processing
Order processing and EDI exchange systems must maintain high availability because downtime directly translates to missed shipments, SLA penalties, and revenue loss. Design for zero-downtime deployments, automatic failover, and real-time monitoring of EDI pipeline health.
