# ONC Health IT Certification

## When to Activate
- When working on healthcare projects that involve ONC Health IT certification criteria, SVAP version adoption, or real-world testing requirements
- When preparing systems for ONC certification or maintaining Conditions of Certification compliance

## Core Principles
### 1. Certification is Continuous, Not One-Time
ONC certification requires ongoing real-world testing, SVAP updates, and Conditions of Certification compliance. Design systems with testability, version management, and compliance reporting built in from the start, not bolted on before certification deadlines.
