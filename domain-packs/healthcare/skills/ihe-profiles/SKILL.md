# IHE Integration Profiles

## When to Activate
- When working on healthcare projects that involve cross-organization document sharing, patient identity matching, or audit trail requirements
- When implementing IHE profiles such as PIX/PDQ for patient identity, MHD/XDS for document sharing, or ATNA for audit

## Core Principles
### 1. Profile Conformance Over Custom Solutions
IHE profiles define tested, interoperable integration patterns. Use the specified actors, transactions, and content modules rather than inventing custom approaches. Conformance enables participation in health information exchanges.
