# Requirements Engineering

## When to Activate
- When gathering, analyzing, or validating requirements

## Core Principles
### 1. Elicitation
Ask why before what. Understand the problem first.

### 2. Acceptance Criteria
Measurable, testable. Use Given-When-Then where possible.

### 3. Traceability
Requirements -> business goals. Stories -> requirements.

## Red Flags
- Vague requirements
- Missing non-functional requirements
